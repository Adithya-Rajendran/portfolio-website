# A continuous experience

The owner requested another design review: the experience page was not working, and the website needs to flow seamlessly. This supersedes the earlier stand-alone planetary widget layout. Keep the automatic JavaScript visuals, warm palette, real CMS content, and the original migration architecture.

The owner approved the generated direction and explicitly requested the same quality across the rest of the pages. Apply the shared editorial hierarchy to About, Contact, project lists/details, writing lists/tags/articles and the missing-page view. Keep each page suited to its task: articles remain quiet and readable; forms stay direct and accessible. This does not call for more animated backgrounds on reading pages.

## Design diagnosis

The light header, full-width dark homepage, light experience heading, boxed planet viewer and separate CV read like different products. The planet dominates the first screen while the experience record is hidden below it. Repeated headings, controls and explanatory disclosures amplify that separation.

## Implementation direction

- Use a consistent dark first-visit theme, retaining explicit saved theme choices and the light option. Maintain shared typography, spacing, compact navigation and an active-page indicator. Transitions should be progressive enhancements that honor reduced motion; preserve ordinary links and browser history.
- Replace the experience widget with a continuous charcoal composition. The heading is “A trajectory of building.” and the eyebrow is “Experience & education”. Quiet PDF links remain easy to find.
- Put factual experience on the first screen beside the planetary scene. On desktop, a spacious numbered timeline occupies roughly 45% and a sticky orbital scene the remaining 55%. Both belong to one background, without a card border. Each chapter contains the actual role, organization, dates, summary, highlights and skills from Sanity. Preserve `#cv` and existing entry anchors.
- Use a thin timeline and clear current-section links. The active chapter may gently cue a world in the visual, but planets are illustrative; do not invent an association with a job or employer. Manual orbit and world selection remain available and must not fight the story updates.
- Keep useful controls compact: world selection, pause, reset and accessible interaction instructions. Remove the redundant “Explore in orbit” introduction, CV disclosure and close-widget framing. The visual starts by default and honors reduced motion. Keep the background visible until the first real frame; do not show a placeholder Earth.
- On mobile, use a compact nonsticky planet scene before the readable timeline. Single-finger vertical scrolling must work through the page. Orbit/zoom should require a deliberate gesture or focus, never trap ordinary page scrolling.
- Include meaningful onward links: Experience to Projects, Projects to Writing and Contact. Keep the rest of the site visually consistent and retain readable long-form articles.
- Validate actual desktop/mobile views, keyboard use, saved themes, navigation history, reduced-motion changes in both directions, no-JavaScript content, context-loss fallback and working orbital controls. Keep the Cloudflare analytics CSP correction narrow.
