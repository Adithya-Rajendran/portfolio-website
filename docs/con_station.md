# The station

The site shows the rooms of Arcadia Station, a station in orbit above Earth.
This concept tells the design of the station, so that the rooms agree with each other.

## The rings

- Four rings, numbered 01 to 04, turn on a center spine `717 km` above Earth.
- Each ring has a radius of `224 m` and turns at `2 rpm`, so a person at the rim has a weight of `1 g`.
- The rim is the floor, and "up" points to the spine.
- A window is in the rim side of a ring, so it shows Earth.
- The ship Ark Aurora, `1,480 m` long, is at a docking port next to the station.
- All rooms of the site are on one part of Ring 04, the Commons deck.

## The deck

The ring is a pressure tube with a radius of `10.5 m`, with its axis `2.3 m` above the deck.
The main deck is a chord of the tube.
The compartments are in a line along the deck, from antispinward to spinward, and pressure bulkheads close each compartment.

| Position | Compartment | Room on the site |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Navigation | Experience |
| 2 | Fabrication Bay 02 | Projects. Its large door is the cargo lift to the Ring Line. |
| 3 | Atrium | Home. The Oculus and the two doors are in its spinward bulkhead. |
| 4 | The bottom of the spoke | No room. It has the lifts to the hub, the stair to the Ring Line and the bottom of the periscope. |
| 5 | Observation Lounge | About. Its window is the second eyepiece of the periscope. |
| 6 | Library | Writing |
| 7 | Comms | Contact |

Below the deck are the Ring Line and the water tanks.
The Ring Line has a tram, a walkway and a freight track through all compartments.
The water tanks are shielding and ballast.

## The view of Earth

A telescope on the hub, which does not turn, points to Earth.
Its light comes down the spoke through a derotator.
At the bottom of the spoke, two mirrors turn the light into the Atrium and into the Lounge.
So, the Oculus of the Atrium and the window of the Lounge are the two eyepieces of one periscope.

## The Atrium

- The cross section of the Atrium is the hull. The hull has a hoop about the tube axis at a pitch of `4 m`.
- Above the balcony, the lining follows the hoops. Below the balcony, flat service walls are in front of the hull.
- The side walls have no doors, because the hull is at the back of them.
- The spinward bulkhead has the two ways out, one on each side of the stair. One is a door to the Lounge, and one is a stair down to the Ring Line.
- The large circle in the spinward bulkhead is the Oculus. It shows the view from the hub telescope through a barrel with baffles.
- The antispinward bulkhead, at the back of the visitor, has the lift lobby from the spoke.
- The visitor can select the hologram and the two doors. The Ring Line door gives a list of stops.
- The deck of the Atrium is flat. On a ring, a deck that follows the ring goes up approximately `0.6 m` at each end of the `33 m` hall.
