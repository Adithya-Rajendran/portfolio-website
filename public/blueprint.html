WHY IT IS BUILT THIS WAY

Round hull. Air pressure wants to make a sphere, so the ring is a circular tube. The ribs in the Atrium are its frames, 4 m apart.

Rooms in a row. A tube only has room for one line of compartments. Each is sealed by bulkheads, so a leak costs one room, and every room has two ways out along the Ring Line.

Dock at the hub. A ship cannot hold on to a moving rim. It docks on the axis, where nothing moves, and people ride a spoke down. That is why the Atrium is at the spoke.

Workshop by the cargo lift. Heavy things arrive weightless at the hub and come down the same spoke, straight into Fabrication.

Water under the floor. The rim is the outside of the station, so tanks there shield the rooms and balance the ring.

No rim windows. The ring turns twice a minute; a window in it would show Earth tumbling. A telescope on the still hub looks at Earth and relays the image down the spoke to the Oculus.

Paired rings. Two pairs turn opposite ways so their spin cancels.
