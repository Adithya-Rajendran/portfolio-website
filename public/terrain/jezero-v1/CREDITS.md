# Credits and data sources

Terrain in this pack is derived from two USGS Astrogeology Science Center products of Jezero crater, Mars (HiRISE-based, 1 m DTM, 0.25 m orthoimage). Derived: resampled, smoothed, re-encoded as PNG/WebP, illumination removed from the orthoimage. Fetched 2026-11-10.
