EXPLORATORY LANDSCAPES & MECHANICAL STUDIES

Homepage: an illustrative Mars landscape assembled, modeled, lit and rendered in Blender/Cycles for this portfolio. It is not a mission photograph or hardware built by the portfolio owner.

Rover geometry: NASA/JPL-Caltech, Mars 2020 Perseverance Rover. Adapted pose and neutral arm graphics. No affiliation or endorsement is implied.
https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/mars-2020-perseverance-rover/
https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

Rock material: Rocks Ground 06, Poly Haven, CC0. Adapted color, scale and bump mapping.
https://polyhaven.com/a/rocks_ground_06
https://polyhaven.com/license

Terrain, composition, lighting, stereo camera and antenna studies: original Blender scenes created for this portfolio. The camera and antenna are illustrative design studies.

The website uses the finished rendered artwork with a depth map for gentle camera motion. The experience page's planets are separate interactive Three.js scenes.
