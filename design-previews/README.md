# Possible Futures — writing-first homepage concept

Branch: `redesign/editorial-rebuild`.

The current preview is `index.html`, authored from scratch in `future.html`. It is a focused homepage concept, not the completed or deployed replacement application.

## Chosen direction

**A living future + Quiet stars**: an inhabited orbital habitat, architectural scale, copper light, deep blue-charcoal, and cream typography. Static stars continue across the entire background with softer contrast behind the writing. Gentle pointer-based image movement respects reduced-motion settings.

The opening is now shorter and specific about Adithya's actual Canonical role and interest in robotic vision. Writing is primary. Work, résumé, LinkedIn, and RSS are easy to find. The published DGX Spark article is a curated starting point, followed by dated recent notes with summaries on both desktop and mobile.

## Content and scope

Published article titles, descriptions, slugs, dates, and profile role were rechecked through the Sanity connector: project `ppk2h9tb`, public `production` dataset, published perspective. No content was written to Sanity. No future articles, benchmarks, robotics projects, qualifications, or availability claims were fabricated.

Navigation currently links to the existing live website. The writing anchor works within the preview. The production application has not been rebuilt or deployed.

## Review and verification

Three independent subagents critiqued the design for readers, recruiters, and founders/CEOs/investors. Editorial and recruiter reviewers then inspected the revised desktop and mobile screenshots. See `design-review.md` for findings, implemented decisions, limitations, and sourced hypothetical Musk/Huang engineering lenses.

Browser checks passed at 320, 390, 768, 1120, 1440, and 1920 px. The featured article is visible in the opening 1120 × 900 view. Artwork loads, text fits, mobile summaries remain visible, stars cover the page, the writing anchor works, and reduced motion is respected. No JavaScript page errors.

## Artwork and previous directions

All future-habitat imagery is original generated concept art, not a project or real location. Original prompts and saved paths are in `artwork-notes.md`.
